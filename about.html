Our Story

Born from Real-World Frustration

Every SAP customer we've worked with has the same problem: their people live in Microsoft Teams, but their approvals live in Fiori. The result? Purchase requisitions sit for days. Procurement stalls. Finance sends reminder emails that nobody reads.

We started OneLoop Labs because we believed connecting SAP to the tools people actually use shouldn't require a six-month consulting engagement. It should be opinionated software with sensible defaults that works out of the box.

Built on Microsoft Azure and grounded in SAP-native architecture, our integrations are designed to survive upgrades, scale with your organization, and ship in weeks — not quarters.
